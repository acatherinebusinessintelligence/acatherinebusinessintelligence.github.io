Research projectOngoing

AgroIA Cormechoque

AI prototype to analyze soil compaction in smallholder farming in the Cormechoque river micro-watershed (Boyacá, Colombia). It combines a technical rules engine, K-Means clustering, PCA, supervised experiments and management scenario simulation, always with human review.

Methodological framework

HUMANO: a human-centered AI framework

A framework to design and audit AI systems across six dimensions: humanize the purpose, locate people and impacts, minimize data and risks, assure autonomy and transparency, name who is accountable, and observe, audit and correct. Demonstration case on the Adult Census dataset.
