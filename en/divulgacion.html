Transparency

How I use AI in what I publish

A short statement, consistent with the HUMANO framework I propose for other systems.

  • A · Transparency. I use generative AI to support research, prototyping and material production: for example, the characters, voice and videos in my courses. When a piece includes AI-generated characters, voices or images, I say so.
  • N · Accountable. Everything I publish is reviewed and adapted by me. AI does not sign my content: I am accountable for it.
  • M · Minimize data. This site uses no analytics or cookies. The page reader uses your own browser's speech voices; the site does not store the text. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • O · Correct. If you find an error or a claim that needs review, write to me and I will fix it.
