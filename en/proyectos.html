Online training program · Healthcare2026

Power BI and Power Automate for healthcare professionals

Pharmaceutical and healthcare company · 2026

I designed and built, end to end, a data analytics and automation training program for professionals at healthcare institutions in Bogotá, Cali and eastern Antioquia.

  • Moodle LMS: instructional design, course structure, modules, cover art and full setup.
  • Interactive H5P content and interactive videos: recorded training each participant takes at their own pace.
  • AI-created materials: characters, voice, video and assessments, reviewed and adapted to the healthcare context.
  • Live group mentoring in Power BI and Power Automate.
  • One-on-one personalized coaching across the three regions, until each participant builds their own dashboard and automates a workflow from their job.
